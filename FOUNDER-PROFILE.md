# GBSBFORYOU — Founder Profile

## Institutional Founder

**Name:** Ajay Singh Chouhan  
**Institutional Role:** Founder, GBSBFORYOU  
**Base:** Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India

## Founder’s Institutional Role

The Founder provides the institutional direction, mission, governance framework and long-term stewardship of GBSBFORYOU.

The Founder is not treated as the officer of every repository. Operational responsibilities may be delegated to Core Officers, Project Officers and Repository Officers while the Founder retains institutional oversight according to the governance framework.

## Mission

To use knowledge, AI and digital technology to develop practical solutions for education, language and culture, research, publishing, media, governance, entrepreneurship and public benefit.

## Working Principles

1. Constitutional and legal compliance.
2. Truth, evidence and transparent documentation.
3. Equal standards for all persons and institutions.
4. Technology for public benefit rather than partisan advocacy.
5. Collaboration across projects and repositories.
6. Clear separation between institutional funds, project funds and personal finances.
7. Every appointed officer has a defined responsibility and a living work profile.
8. Every repository has a defined role within the Master Platform.
9. Public support should be connected to clearly identified work and documented outputs.
10. Repositories may evolve, split, be renamed or become successors without being treated automatically as separate missions.

## Officer Profile Principle

Every appointed officer will have a profile containing role, appointment, responsibilities, current projects, completed work, skills, milestones, reports and work history. The profile is intended to be updated during service.

## Repository Team Principle

A repository is an accountable work unit, not an isolated mission. Each REP may have its own technical responsibility while collaborating with other REP units under the common GBSBFORYOU mission and standards.

## Public Accountability

GBSBFORYOU will distinguish facts from claims, document project outputs, preserve repository lineage and avoid presenting prototypes, concepts or historical repositories as completed institutional achievements.

## Status

This profile is the working institutional profile of the Founder and may be updated as the GBSBFORYOU governance system develops.
